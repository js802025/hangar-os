Trek Domane AL 2 review

A comfortable, forgiving aluminium road bike that's easy to live with. ★★★★☆

The goodRelaxed endurance geometry, room for 32 mm tyres, mudguard mounts. Great for commuting and long weekend rides.
The not so goodClaris 8-speed shifting is basic, and the stock wheels are heavy. Worth upgrading tyres first.
Used buying tipPre-2020 models had a known creak at the seat post; from 2021 it was fixed. Check the chain for stretch and ask about service history.
Compared withLighter and racier: Specialized Allez. Cheaper and older but stiff: Cannondale CAAD8.
VerdictThe best all-rounder under $500 second-hand, especially a 2021 or later.
