Buying a used road bike

A good second-hand road bike costs a third of a new one, if you know what to check.

Get the size rightFrame size matters more than components. Most riders around 5'10" fit a 56 cm frame.
Aluminium is the sweet spotUnder $500, an aluminium frame with Shimano Sora or Claris is the best value.
Ask for a test rideCheck the chain, the brake pads and the wheels for wobble before you pay.
