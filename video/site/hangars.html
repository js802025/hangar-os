The great airship hangars

Built for the age of the zeppelin, a handful still stand, and they're among the largest single rooms ever made.

Why so big?A rigid airship like the USS Macon was 239 m long. The hangar had to hold it, its handling crew and the winter air that kept it from drifting.
Where they are nowFilm sets, space-research labs, an indoor tropical beach. Most are listed buildings today.
