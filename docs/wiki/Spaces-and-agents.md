# Spaces and agents

A **space** is a computer of its own for one job: a desktop with a browser,
apps and a workspace folder. An **agent** is an AI working on that desktop.
A space can hold a whole crew, each agent with its own mouse, keyboard and
browser tab, working at the same time.

![Two agents at work in one space](images/space.png)

## Watching

Your spaces are listed under the chats in Hangar, with live thumbnails. Click
one to open it beside the conversation: its live desktop (each agent's
cursor in its own colour, with its name), its **crew** (who's doing what), and
**Changes** (see [Reviewing changes](Reviewing-changes)).

## Every space at once

Press **Meta+S** (or the Mission Control key on an Apple keyboard), or the ⌗
button in Hangar, to see every space live. Click one to open it; Esc to leave.

![The overview](images/overview.png)

## Stepping in

**Take over** gives you the space's mouse and keyboard: the agents pause
while you do something yourself (solve a puzzle, check a detail, finish a
form). **Hand back** and they carry on.

![Taking over](images/takeover.png)

## A space on its own desktop

**Own desktop** moves a space full screen onto a KDE virtual desktop of its
own, named after it. Switch to it like any desktop (Meta+Ctrl+arrows, or the
pager); close it and the desktop goes away.

## Giving a space a task yourself

Type into **Give … a task** under the space. Hangar saves the workspace first,
so whatever happens can be [undone](Reviewing-changes).

## What agents can use

Every space has a browser, a terminal and a text editor. Spaces can also come
with extra apps:

| kind | adds |
|---|---|
| research | PDF and image viewers |
| coding | compilers, Python tools, GitHub CLI, code editors |
| office | LibreOffice Writer, Calc and Impress |

Agents read desktop apps through the accessibility system, much like a
screen reader, so they work with real buttons and fields rather than guessing
from pixels.

## Folders

A space can't see your files unless you share a folder with it, read-only by
default. See [Privacy and security](Privacy-and-security).
