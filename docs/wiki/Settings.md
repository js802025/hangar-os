# Settings

Open **Hangar Settings** from the app menu (or the tray icon).

| section | what you set |
|---|---|
| **Orchestration** | the pilot's model; which models it may give agents; the most agents running at once; whether spaces it creates get open internet or only the sites it names |
| **Ask Hangar** | voice input: download a speech model (tiny to medium), or remove it |
| **Notes** | what agents may read in your notes (see [Notes](Notes)) |
| **Notifications** | ntfy, Slack and Telegram, with a test button |
| **Access** | opening Hangar from other devices on your network (see [From another device](From-another-device)) |
| **Run setup again** | the first-boot wizard |

AI accounts and local models have their own apps: **Accounts** and **Models**
(see [AI accounts and models](AI-accounts-and-models)).
